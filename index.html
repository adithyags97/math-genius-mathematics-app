Course information


Course outcomes
  • CO1: Multivariable and vector calculus: derivatives, optimization, vector fields.
  • CO2: Solve linear systems; eigenvalues and eigenvectors by direct and iterative methods.
  • CO3: Vector spaces and linear transformations in CS engineering.
  • CO4: Applications using modern ICT tools.


Assessment
  • CIE 50 + SEE 50 = 100 marks, 3 hr exam.
  • CIE = average of 2 IA tests (25) + CCA (25).
  • CCA Activity-1: two objective tests, 7.5 each (15). Activity-2: lab or group seminar (max 4 students), 10 marks.
  • Need 20/50 in CIE to be eligible, 18/50 in SEE, and 40/100 overall.


Textbooks
  • B. S. Grewal, Higher Engineering Mathematics, 44th Ed.
  • E. Kreyszig, Advanced Engineering Mathematics, 10th Ed.
  • Lipschutz & Lipson, Linear Algebra (Schaum's), 4th Ed.
  • Refs: Strang, Lay, Poole, Stewart, Ramana, Bali & Goyal, Williams.

  • MIT OCW 18.06 Linear Algebra, 18.02SC Multivariable Calculus
  • VTU e-Shikshana, VTU EDUSAT, academicearth.org
